Brother Network terms of use

Updated October 30, 2026

The Phi Gamma Delta Sigma Mu Brother Network supports chapter communication and connections among Active and Graduate brothers. Please follow these rules when requesting access or using the network.

Membership and accounts

Provide accurate registration and profile information and use an account that belongs to you. All new accounts require chapter approval. Google sign-in verifies your Google identity; it does not automatically establish chapter membership or grant access to the directory.

Respectful use

Use the network for legitimate chapter and brotherhood purposes. Respect other members' privacy. Do not scrape or redistribute the directory, use contact details for spam, impersonate another person, share account access, or attempt to bypass access controls. Only upload or post material you have permission to share.

Chapter administration

Chapter administrators manage membership requests, member records, and chapter content. Contact chapter leadership about access decisions, inaccurate records, or inappropriate content.

Information and support

See the privacy notice for how account and profile information is used and displayed. Questions about the website can be sent to chandlerchambliss@icloud.com.
