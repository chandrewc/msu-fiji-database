Brother Network privacy

Updated October 30, 2026

This notice describes how the Phi Gamma Delta Sigma Mu Brother Network at brothers.msufiji.com uses information for chapter registration, member profiles, and the brother directory.

Account and profile information

When you use Google sign-in, Google shares your account identifier, email address, and basic profile information, such as your name and profile picture, with our authentication provider, Supabase. We use this information to identify your account and help you complete registration. We request only basic sign-in information, not access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or calendars. The website does not receive your Google password.

Registration also asks for your name, username, contact email, phone number, and Active or Graduate status. You can add other brother profile details, including graduation year, pledge class, location, employment, biography, networking preferences, and a photo. Email/password registration remains available through Supabase.

How information is used and shared

Chapter administrators use account and profile information to review membership requests and administer the network. Approval is required before member access is granted. Approved brothers can view other approved brothers' directory profiles, including contact details and other profile information provided for the directory. Please provide information you are comfortable sharing with approved members.

Supabase stores account, profile, and website data and manages authentication. Account confirmation and password-reset messages use the configured email service, Resend. Newsletter emails are currently paused; newsletters are posted on the website. Where donation checkout is offered, Stripe handles payment details; the website does not collect full payment-card numbers.

Sessions and account records

The website stores authentication session information in your browser to keep you signed in. Signing out ends the website session on that browser. Supabase and connected services process the technical records needed to operate authentication and the website. Chapter administration also records administrative activity.

Your information

You can update available profile fields in My Profile. For account corrections, removal requests, or questions about information visible to other brothers, contact chapter leadership or chandlerchambliss@icloud.com. You can also remove the Google connection through your Google account settings; this does not by itself delete your Brother Network account or profile.

We will update this notice when the website's information practices change.
